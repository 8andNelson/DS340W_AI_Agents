Overview

Enter a research topic, or supply a fixed Parent Paper to compare modeling runs. The Master Agent supervises each stage and runs independent work in parallel.

Optional. Skips the paper search and uses your paper (text-based PDF, up to 50 MB).

Research and Validation are skipped. Leave the title or prediction target empty and the Intake Agent reads it from the PDF. Reuse the same PDF, title, topic and prediction target for matching inputs.
